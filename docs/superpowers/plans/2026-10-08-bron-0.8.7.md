## Findings this plan answers (the spec)

1. **One page for many documents.** Today a document page names one document (`doc: "<id>"`), and the check lists every read document without a page. A set of near-identical documents (70 share-transfer agreements in one folder, the same form signed by different people) and copies/drafts of a document that already has a page each needed their own page. → `doc:` may be a list; every listed document counts as having that page.
2. **Pages that keep growing.** A page that gains a line for every document read (the user's own organisation, a person who signs everything, a law firm, an entity that holds many things) hit the 20,000-character limit and was trimmed to just under it in 8 of 10 runs, losing detail each time. → When flagged, tidy to about 15,000; the skill says how such pages stay short (roles + one line per item linking the item's page; a long list moves to its own topic page).
3. **Checks the full checkup needs** (domain-neutral): summaries too long for `index.md` (median 302 characters, 208 over 400; `index.md` 345 KB); notes saying something "isn't checked yet" that outlive the check; raw spreadsheet numbers on summary pages (12192630, 19389341.05263158); timelines out of date order. The first is a real problem (summaries are what search and `index.md` show); the other three can be false alarms, so they're a "worth a look" list, not problems `wiki done` must clear.
4. **Background-reading messages.** "I'll report when it's done" is wrong in Codex (it can't wake a conversation); "I'll add these after it (12 min)" was read as a 12-minute wait before starting; a Codex conversation polled with `sleep` and re-read documents the background run was writing. Ticket titles use only the last folder name ("Read the Series A folder" for four companies).
5. **Skill text.** The shell rule says variables and loops stop a background run; they don't (`--allowedTools Bash`). The `=====` rule is missing from the reader helper. Reader helpers saved nothing ($0.559 vs $0.556 per doc) because the main agent re-reads what readers read.
6. **Search speed.** Every search rescans the wiki with `Path.stat` (29 ms at 1,100 pages; `os.scandir` measured 2.4 ms) and fetches page excerpts with `WHERE path=? AND w=?` on an FTS table (a full scan, 13 ms). A search right after a page edit rewrites `index.md` (650 ms) and walks every stored document.
7. **Search ranking.** Measured on 22 real questions: right document in top 3 = 17, top 10 = 19. Clause numbers ("Section 10.9") are buried by OR'd common words; 26% of passages are word-for-word copies in other documents (LPA versions, redlines), so a top 10 held 6.2 different documents. Stopwords + a phrase ranking for numbers with their preceding word + at most 2 passages per document + collapsing identical passages gave top 3 = 19, top 10 = 21, 8.0 documents per top 10, 0 repeated passages. Output averages 13.6K characters per search; showing the best ~600-character window cuts ~40%.
